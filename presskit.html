About

Bumpo is car football on a frozen rink. Red against blue, the cars drift, the ball bounces off the boards, and every match turns into a mess of near misses, lucky bounces and last second goals.

It is built around two people sharing one phone. Put it flat on the table, one player at each end, and play. No wifi, no account, no ads. 30 perks drop onto the ice during a match: freeze your rival, set the ball on fire, turn the lights off, drop an oil spill in their path.

Features

  • Two players on one phone, each driving from their own end of the screen
  • 30 perks that change the match: freeze, fireball, dark mode, magnet, bomb, party and more
  • Instant replay of every goal, shareable as a clip with sound
  • Joystick or old school button controls
  • Trophy room with driver ranks and a road trip from the Eiffel Tower to the Moon
