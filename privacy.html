Privacy Policy

Bumpo · Last updated September 21, 2026

Bumpo is a car football game. It is built to collect as little as possible. This page explains exactly what happens with data when you play.

No accounts, no personal data

Bumpo has no sign up and no login. It does not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location or any other personal information.

Stored on your iPhone

Your settings and game stats (wins, goals, distance driven, trophies) are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Anonymous usage statistics

To understand how the game is played and to improve it, Bumpo sends anonymous usage signals to TelemetryDeck, a privacy focused analytics service based in the European Union. Examples: a match was started, a match finished with a score, a button was tapped. These signals contain no personal information, cannot be used to identify you, are not linked to your identity and are never used for advertising or tracking across apps or websites.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center so they can appear on leaderboards. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. Bumpo does not receive your Apple ID details.

Purchases

The 2 Players unlock is bought through the App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple. Bumpo never sees your payment details.

Sharing goal clips

When you share a goal clip, the video is created on your iPhone and sent only where you choose through the iOS share menu. Bumpo does not upload it anywhere.

No ads, no tracking

Bumpo shows no ads and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Children

Bumpo is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
