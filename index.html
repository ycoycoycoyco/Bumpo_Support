Frequently asked

How do I play with a friend?

Choose 2 PLAYERS on the main menu. Red plays from the bottom of the screen, blue from the top, both on the same iPhone. 2 Players is a one time in-app purchase.

I bought 2 Players. How do I get it on a new iPhone?

Tap the 2 PLAYERS tab and choose Restore purchase, using the same Apple ID. With Family Sharing, your family members get it too.

Can I change the controls?

Yes. Open Settings to switch between joystick and buttons, change steering sensitivity, and turn vibration on or off.

Where are my stats and trophies?

Tap the trophy next to the odometer on the main menu. Leaderboards and achievements are in Game Center.
